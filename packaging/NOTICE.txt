@NAME@ runs the filters of the OBS Studio you installed. It ships no OBS code:
when it runs, it loads OBS’s libobs from a private copy of your install. Its
source includes declarations from OBS Studio’s libobs headers, under their own
notices: the GNU General Public License, version 2 or later, or the ISC license.

The installer can also install VB-CABLE, a virtual cable by VB-Audio
Software, when the PC has no virtual cable. It carries VB-CABLE’s driver pack
as VB-Audio ships it, and runs VB-Audio’s own installer, as VB-Audio allows
(https://vb-audio.com/Services/licensing.htm). VB-CABLE comes from
www.vb-cable.com, and isn’t covered by the GPL. VB-CABLE is a donationware,
all participations are welcome. Uninstalling @NAME@ leaves it installed.
